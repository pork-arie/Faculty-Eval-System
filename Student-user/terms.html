Terms of Use

BETA — FOR TESTING ONLY
This is a beta version. This system is a capstone project of students of the College of Computing and Information Sciences, Northwest Samar State University. It is being tested and is not yet an official evaluation system of the University.

1. Purpose of this beta

This Faculty Performance Evaluation System was developed as a capstone project and is being tested to confirm that it works as intended and follows CHED Memorandum Order No. 19, series of 2025. Your participation helps the researchers find problems before the system is proposed for official use.

2. What your ratings are used for

  • Ratings submitted during this beta are used to test the system.
  • They are not used for any official faculty evaluation, ranking, promotion or reclassification decision.
  • Test data may be reset or deleted at any time, including at the end of the testing period.

3. Confidentiality of your responses

Your individual ratings and comments are not shown to the faculty member you evaluate, and the system does not display your name beside them. Results are reported in summary form only, consistent with CMO 19 §6.10.

4. Information collected

To let you sign in and to list the subjects you are enrolled in, the system uses your ID number, name, course, year, section and department, together with the ratings and optional comments you submit. This information is handled in line with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173). It is used only for this capstone testing, accessible only to the researchers and the designated evaluation office, and deleted when testing ends.

5. Your responsibilities

  • Use only your own account, and do not share your password.
  • Rate honestly and fairly, based on your own experience in the class.
  • Do not enter offensive, threatening or personal remarks in comments.
  • Do not attempt to access other users' accounts or data, or to disrupt the system.

6. No guarantee during testing

Because this is a beta, the system may contain errors, be unavailable at times, or change without notice. The researchers will do their best to keep it working, but it is provided as-is for testing purposes.

7. Reporting a problem

If something does not work, looks wrong, or you have a concern about your data, please contact the researchers: Ariel Angel Bangcale,B-Yan M. Galecio,Johnrey A. Burca,Andrealyn A. Sildo at Anghel@gmail.com. Your feedback directly improves the system.

8. Agreement

By signing in, you confirm that you understand this is a beta system used for capstone testing, and that you agree to these terms.
